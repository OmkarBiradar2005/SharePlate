How It Works

1️⃣

Restaurant Posts Food

Restaurants with excess food create a donation listing with details like quantity, type, pickup location, and expiry time.

2️⃣

NGO Accepts Donation

NGOs browse available donations and accept the ones they can collect and distribute to those in need.

3️⃣

Food Gets Distributed

NGOs pick up the food and mark the donation as completed once it's distributed to the community.
